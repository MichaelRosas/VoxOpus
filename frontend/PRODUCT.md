# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Students managing tasks and reminders. The user requested a calm, focused, approachable interface.

## Product Purpose
VoxOpus is a CS423 speech to-do and reminder project. The current prototype supports manual task creation, completion, editing, deletion, due dates, reminders, and local storage.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current main branch supports speech dictation, typed commands, LLM interpretation, and proposed-change confirmation alongside manual task controls. This pass refines presentation and adds only the explicitly requested recording-start and recording-stop earcons. Preserve all other behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body and control text at least 16px; text contrast at least 4.5:1; visible keyboard focus and readable mobile layouts.

## Evidence on Hand
Project outline, Audio and Speech Interaction lecture, JustShape paper, and Speech Calendar user-defined study notes supplied by the user. The notes contain examples for setting and correcting dates, not a complete study dataset. JustShape is a CAD study, not validation of this task manager.

## Product Principles
- Make available actions explicit.
- Keep tasks and their dates visible and easy to correct.
- Preserve manual control.
- Keep input short and reduce visual distraction.
